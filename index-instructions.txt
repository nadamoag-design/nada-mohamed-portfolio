INTEGRATION STEPS

1) Upload portfolio-upgrade.css and portfolio-upgrade.js to the repository (for example, into an `assets` folder).
2) In index.html, inside <head>, add this line after your existing stylesheet link:
   <link rel="stylesheet" href="assets/portfolio-upgrade.css">
3) Before the closing </body> tag, add:
   <script src="assets/portfolio-upgrade.js" defer></script>
4) If you upload the two files to the repository root instead of `assets`, remove `assets/` from both paths.
5) Commit the changes, wait for GitHub Pages to deploy, then hard-refresh the site.

Do not replace your existing styles.css or script.js with these enhancement files.
